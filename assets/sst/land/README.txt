The /sst coastline (land-*.json.gz, format GOLAND1)

Built by tools/build_land.py from the OpenStreetMap land polygons published by osmdata.openstreetmap.de
(https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/data/land-polygons.html): clipped to the SST coverage box (37.8 to 44.0 N,
76.0 to 64.8 W), simplified to three levels of detail, cut into 1-degree tiles and quantized.
